HitMark on a phone

Moving a shot

  • Open the target with 'Move' chosen at the top. Touch a hit and it should turn blue to show it is selected; keep your finger down while a magnifier shows the spot in the preview
  • Slide the hit to its correct place
  • Lift your finger when the circle sits exactly on the hole. You can keep refining the position as long as your finger stays down. The score and statistics update straight away, and the hit stays blue until it is deselected.

Deleting a shot

  • Select a hit to delete the same way as above
  • Tap 'Delete hit' above the picture
  • The shot is now marked as a miss, and you will see '–' at the bottom of the list

Adding a shot

  • Choose 'Add shot' just above the picture (any shots that are still missing are displayed with a '–' symbol in the list of shots)
  • Touch the preview where the hole is and, holding your finger down, slide to refine its location, then lift it.

HitMark on a computer

Moving a shot

  • First select the hit you want to move. To do this, click it with the mouse (it turns blue) and keep the button held down; a magnifier shows the spot under the pointer. Scroll the mouse wheel to zoom in on the picture; drag an empty part to move around
  • Drag the hit to its new place and let go of the button. For fine adjustments use the arrow keys: each press moves the selected hit a little, Shift moves further.

Deleting a shot

  • Select a hit to delete the same way as above
  • Press the Delete key on your keyboard or click 'Delete hit'
  • The shot is now marked as missing, and you will see the score '–' for it in the shot list

Adding a shot

  • Choose 'Add shot' at the top of the picture; any shots still missing show '–' in the list. Made a mistake? Click 'Undo' or press Ctrl+Z to take back the change
  • Click on the picture where the hole is and keep the button held down to fine-tune it, then let go of it

If many shots were found in the wrong places, it may be quicker to click 'Clear all' in the app and then mark each of them again by hand.
