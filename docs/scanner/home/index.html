ShootingScanner
Target Scoring System in your browser!

ShootingScanner turns your iPhone, iPad, Android phone or laptop into a target scoring system. Just take a photo of your paper target and the app will automatically find every shot hole and calculate the score! ShootingScanner will also help you analyse your shooting group, calculating windage, elevation, mean radius and extreme spread, so you can easily find out whether to adjust your sights or which pellets are best for you. Keep a record of all your sessions, see each hit on one target and a heat map, and monitor your progress. It costs nothing!

Features

Analyse Shooting Group

Windage and elevation in millimetres, mean radius, extreme spread and group size for every card and the whole session.

Flexible

10 m air pistol and air rifle, 25 m precision / 50 m pistol and 50 m rifle targets, with any number of shots per card.

Keep record of your past sessions

Every session and photo stays in your browser. Save a backup file whenever you like and restore it on another device.

Monitor your progress

See all hits of a session on one target, flick through each card, and spot patterns on the heat map over 7, 30 or 90 days.

Portable

Works on phones, tablets and computers. On a phone the camera opens straight away; on a computer just upload the photo.
